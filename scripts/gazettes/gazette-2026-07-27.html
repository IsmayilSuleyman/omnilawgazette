Lead Story Azerbaijan Switches On Its First Crowdfunding Regime Decree of 24 July 2026 — Law No. 450-VIIQ

The defining instrument of the week is the Presidential Decree of 24 July 2026 applying the Law "On Crowdfunding" (Kraudfandinq haqqında), No. 450-VIIQ of 14 July 2026 — the Republic's first dedicated statutory framework for investment- and lending-based crowdfunding platforms. The decree is the trigger that converts an adopted statute into a live regulatory project, and it hands the substantive rulemaking to the Central Bank of the Republic of Azerbaijan.

Under the decree, the President reserves to himself the powers arising under Articles 3.2 and 18.8 of the Law, rather than delegating them to an executive authority. The Cabinet of Ministers is given four months to bring presidential acts into conformity with the Law and resolve other implementation questions, and three months to determine the offshore zones to which the Law's restrictions will attach.

The Central Bank has six months to adopt the regulatory acts that will decide whether the market is workable: operator capital requirements and minimum aggregate capital thresholds; technical standards for crowdfunding platforms; per-project and per-platform investment ceilings for individual investors; the funding thresholds above which professional-investor participation becomes mandatory; investor complaint procedures; and reporting forms and filing procedure.

Why this matters for the firm: the six-month Central Bank window is the consultation period in practice. Fintech, capital-markets and banking clients contemplating a platform launch should be positioning now — the capital and investment-cap parameters, once fixed, will determine the viability of retail-facing models. Expect draft CBAR acts to circulate well before the January 2027 outer limit.
Office of the President Fərmanlar — Decrees 23–24 July 2026 — president.az
Decree 24 July 2026
Application of the Law "On Crowdfunding" No. 450-VIIQ of 14 July 2026
Brings the new crowdfunding statute into operation. Powers under Arts. 3.2 and 18.8 retained by the President. Cabinet: four months to align presidential acts; three months to designate offshore zones. Central Bank: six months to issue six categories of implementing regulation (see lead story).
Decree 24 July 2026
Implementation of Law No. 469-VIIQD of 14 July 2026 amending the Law "On Execution"
Amends Presidential Decree No. 672 of 5 March 2002 on the application of the Law "On Execution": the reference to the second sentence of Article 27.4 is deleted from paragraph 2.1, and paragraph 2.2-1 is repealed in full. Part one of Presidential Decree No. 406 of 5 June 2025 — which had implemented Law No. 196-VIIQD of 16 May 2025 on civil procedure and execution — is also repealed.
Decree 24 July 2026
Application of Law No. 464-VIIQD of 14 July 2026 amending the Laws "On Medical Insurance" and "On the Rights of Persons with Disabilities"
A structural reorganisation of mandatory medical insurance. Implementing authority passes from the State Agency on Mandatory Health Insurance to the Ministry of Labour and Social Protection of the Population, with a new State Medical Insurance and Expertise Agency established within the Ministry. A "Mandatory Medical Insurance" subsystem is to be formed within the Ministry's centralised electronic information system by 1 January 2027. The Cabinet has three months to submit draft rules on the classification and investment of MMI fund reserves. Conforming amendments are made to Decrees No. 502/2019 and No. 634/2019.
Decree 24 July 2026
Amendment to the Statute of the Ministry of Economy
Amends the "Statute of the Ministry of Economy of the Republic of Azerbaijan" approved by Presidential Decree No. 911 of 30 December 2019. Institutional-competence change; the consolidated text should be checked on e-qanun.az before relying on any allocation of ministerial functions.
Decree 23 July 2026
Application of Law No. 444-VIIQD of 13 July 2026 amending the Law "On Psychiatric Care"
Puts the psychiatric care amendments into effect and amends Presidential Decree No. 574 of 3 September 2001 on the application of the Law "On Psychiatric Care". Relevant to health-sector regulatory work and to capacity, guardianship and involuntary-treatment questions.
Decree 23 July 2026
Application of Law No. 432-VIIQD of 30 June 2026 amending the Housing Code and the legislation on refugees and internally displaced persons
Implements the housing and IDP-status amendment package adopted at the close of the spring session. Read together with the Presidential Order of the same date amending the State Programme on refugees and IDPs (Section 2), this is the second of two instruments touching resettlement entitlements this week.

Regulatory Spotlight — Key Themes, Week 31 / 2026

1. A New Market Opens — Crowdfunding

Law 450-VIIQ is the first Azerbaijani statute to regulate crowdfunding as a distinct activity, and the 24 July decree starts the clock on the Central Bank's rulemaking. Until the CBAR acts land, the perimeter — who counts as an operator, what capital they must hold, what a retail investor may commit — is undefined. Corporate, banking and fintech teams should be scoping platform structures now and treating the next two quarters as the window in which the commercial parameters of this market are settled.

2. Health Insurance Administration Restructured

The 24 July decree on Law 464-VIIQD moves mandatory medical insurance out of the standalone State Agency and into the Ministry of Labour and Social Protection, creating a State Medical Insurance and Expertise Agency inside the Ministry. This is a change of counterparty as much as of competence: employers, insurers and providers should identify every contract, filing and correspondence channel currently addressed to the Agency. The 1 January 2027 systems deadline is the practical migration date.

3. Enforcement Framework Pruned

The decree implementing Law 469-VIIQD strips provisions from Decree No. 672/2002 and repeals part of Decree No. 406/2025 — the implementing scaffolding around the Law "On Execution" is being simplified rather than extended. Litigation and debt-recovery practitioners should re-check any enforcement strategy that relied on the deleted paragraph 2.2-1 or on the second sentence of Article 27.4, and should not assume the 2025 implementing provisions still stand.

4. Liberated Territories — From Programme to Institution

Cabinet Decision No. 236 approves model structures and staff units for the enterprises delivering restoration, construction and management services in the liberated territories, while the Presidential Order of 23 July amends the 2004 State Programme on refugees and IDPs and the decree of the same date implements the Housing Code / IDP-status amendments. Three instruments in three days: the reconstruction effort is acquiring a standing administrative architecture, and the counterparties to reconstruction contracts are being formalised.
