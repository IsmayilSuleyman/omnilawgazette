İqtisadi Kabinet / Nazirlər Kabineti Cabinet of Ministers Decisions & Orders — 9–13 July 2026

The Cabinet of Ministers issued ten Decisions (Nos. 205–214) and five Orders during the reporting period, concentrated on 9–10 July with a final decision (No. 211) dated 13 July. The 10 July batch of five Orders reads as a coordinated defense- and land-sector implementation push. Decisions Nos. 205–207 are already consolidated on e-qanun.az; the remainder are drawn from the Cabinet's own register.

Regulatory Spotlight — Key Themes, 6–13 July 2026

1. Land Code Reform Continues

Two Cabinet Orders on 10 July (482s, 483s) further implement and modify the Land Code, and two Milli Majlis bills tabled the same day (items 16–17 above) propose additional Land Code / Civil Code amendments touching cadastre, land monitoring, the land market, and agricultural insurance. Together with June's Law No. 420-VIIQD, this marks a sustained multi-month land tenure reform effort. Practitioners advising on real estate, agricultural land, or land-backed financing should track this closely.

2. Defense & Conscription Framework Overhaul

Orders 484s–486s (10 July) implement a new Territorial Defense charter, Forest Protection Law amendments, and the Military Service Charter/conscription law in a single session. Combined with the Majlis's own defense-adjacent bill activity, this suggests an active government push on national defense administration this quarter.

3. Omnibus Legislative Sitting — 19 Bills in One Day

The 10 July Majlis sitting tabled 19 bills spanning tax, customs, procurement, agriculture (seed production, breeding, fisheries), healthcare, and codes of criminal and administrative procedure. This is an unusually large single-day slate and likely reflects pre-recess clearing of the legislative docket. Firms with cross-practice interests (tax, customs, agribusiness, healthcare) should flag this sitting for close monitoring through subsequent readings.

4. Energy: Georgia Agreements & New Gas Rules

Two presidential decrees on 9 July ratify intergovernmental agreements with Georgia on natural gas supply and on electricity supply and transit — reinforcing Azerbaijan's role as a regional energy corridor. In parallel, Cabinet Decision No. 206 replaces the 2011 Gas Usage Rules outright, and No. 205 introduces building energy-efficiency passportization. Energy, construction, and real-estate practices should review the new rules closely.

5. Compliance-Relevant Cabinet Decisions

Decision No. 210 (non-resident financial transaction reporting) and No. 213 (NGO annual financial report format) affect ongoing reporting obligations for financial institutions and civil-society organizations respectively; No. 211 (13 July) makes conforming amendments implementing Law 335-VIIQD and Decree 593. Compliance teams should review the full texts once gazetted on e-qanun.az.
