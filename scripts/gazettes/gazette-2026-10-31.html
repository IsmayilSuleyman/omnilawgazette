Konstitusiya Məhkəməsi Constitutional Court Decisions — 24–30 Oct 2026
PlenumNo. 1634 26 Oct 2026
Interpretation of Arts. 66.5 and 81.2 of the Criminal Code in light of Arts. 60 and 71 of the Constitution
Referred by a Baku district court. Holding: the part of a sentence from which a person was released by amnesty must not be counted in aggregating (subsuming) sentences under Art. 66.5; amnesty has real legal effect, and a fully released person gives no basis for an aggregate sentence on that earlier judgment. Final; effective on publication. Source

Regulatory Spotlight — Key Themes, 24–30 Oct 2026

Autumn legislative wave

20 bills tabled on 27 and 30 October, including the 2027 State Budget, new laws on Free Legal Aid and on protection of corruption whistleblowers, and amendments to the Labour, Civil, Criminal, Migration and procedural codes.

Digitalisation of state services

The State Services implementing law, the DERKİS real-estate registry regulation, and Cabinet Decision 361 all push filings and requests onto EHİS and e-cabinets.

Amnesty and sentencing

Constitutional Court Decision 1634: amnestied portions of a sentence are excluded from aggregation under Criminal Code Art. 66.5.

Protected areas & environment

Karabakh National Park, Azıx-Tağlar Archaeological Park reserve, sanitary zones for Axar-Baxar and Ilisu parks, and EIA-related amendments.
