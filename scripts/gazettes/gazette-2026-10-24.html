Decrees (Fərmanlar) & Laws signed

Decree 22 Oct 2026
Technology Parks Model Regulation (Decree No. 168 of 2014) — Amended
Freelancers and foreign "digital nomads" earning income from abroad may now register as technopark residents; a new "non-resident" category covers entities leasing park infrastructure. Clause 8 on resident activities is fully replaced (AI, cloud, cybersecurity, blockchain, gaming, IoT, robotics added; 7-working-day registration; seven-member expert council). Cabinet must revise the expert-council regulation within 3 months. Effective 1 Jan 2027; existing residents must re-register by that date to keep certain tax benefits. source ↗
Decree 22 Oct 2026
Implementation of Law No. 476-VIIQD (Tax, Migration, Social Insurance, Inspections, Procurement)
Allocates executive-body powers under the Tax Code (arts. 101.7.1, 102.1.21, 106.1.13, 113.2, 199.7, 207.3), the Social Insurance Law (art. 14.9.1) and the State Procurement Law (arts. 1.3, 46.4-1, 46.4-2). Cabinet to approve, within 3 months, thresholds for large technopark residents, non-cash R&D expense rules and innovation-product procurement procedures. Amends the ASAN Visa regulation so e-visa data of digital nomads is transmitted in real time to the Ministry of Digital Development and Transport and the State Migration Service. The law itself was signed the same day (see below). source ↗

Konstitusiya Məhkəməsi Constitutional Court Decisions — 17–24 Oct 2026
DecreeDecision 20 Oct 2026
Interpretation of Article 157.1 of the Criminal Procedure Code (judicial control over detention)
On a Prosecutor's Office referral (arts. 156.1, 346.1, 447.5 CPC; Constitution arts. 28 and 60). The Court held that, when ruling on a detention application, a court must examine: (i) reasonable suspicion linking the person to the offence — not merely that an offence occurred; (ii) sufficiency of initial evidence in concrete case materials rather than abstract assumptions; (iii) the legal grounds for detention; and (iv) whether a lighter preventive measure suffices. Courts may request additional materials from investigators but must not prejudge guilt; a refusal for insufficient evidence does not bar a fresh application on new facts. Takes effect on publication; final. source ↗

Regulatory Spotlight — Key Themes, 17–24 Oct 2026

1. Digital-Nomad & Technopark Regime

The 22 Oct package (Law 476-VIIQD, the implementing Decree, and the amended Technology Parks Model Regulation) opens technopark residency to freelancers and digital nomads, adds a non-resident category, and ties tax, migration (e-visa data sharing) and procurement rules together. Key date: 1 Jan 2027 re-registration; Cabinet acts due within three months. Relevant to tax, immigration, IT and start-up clients.

2. 2027 State Budget and Autumn Bill Wave

The 2027 budget bill and eleven other bills were tabled on 23 Oct, including amendments to the Criminal and Criminal Procedure Codes, Labour Code, Civil Code (twice), Enforcement Law, State Procurement Law and a new Law on Product Certificates. Cabinet Decision No. 353 already implements the 2026 Labour Code amendments.

3. Healthcare Reshuffle

Decree of 19 Oct moves TƏBİB under the Ministry of Health, with a new Minister (A. Bayramov) and T. Musayev as Deputy PM; Order No. 794s and Decision No. 354 (compulsory health insurance) follow. Watch for charter and contracting changes affecting medical-insurance clients.

4. Constitutional Court on Pre-Trial Detention

The 20 Oct interpretation of CPC art. 157.1 sets a clearer test for detention hearings — person-specific reasonable suspicion, concrete evidence, and mandatory consideration of lighter measures. Directly relevant to criminal defence practice.

5. Export Support & Low-Carbon Strategy

Decisions 349–351 set origin rules and exclusion lists for export-clearance and transport support; Decision 346 adopts the Long-Term Low-Carbon Development Strategy; Decision 345 extends solar-plant permit periods.

6. Liberated Territories: Protected Areas

Karabakh National Park (88,418 ha) and the Azykh-Taglar State Reserve continue the creation of specially protected areas; Cabinet deadlines (3 months for Azykh-Taglar) will generate implementing acts.
