Regulatory Spotlight — Key Themes, 5 – 26 Sep 2026

1. 2027 Draft Budget Tabled

Cabinet Decision No. 312 (25 Sep) approves the draft 2027 state budget and macroeconomic forecast indicators for submission to parliament — normally the single most consequential fiscal-policy event of the autumn cycle. Watch for the Milli Majlis's autumn-session budget debate and any related tax-law amendments once the draft is tabled there.

2. Labour & Social-Insurance Implementation Continues

Decision No. 307 (21 Sep) amends implementing decisions tied to the ongoing Labour Code and pension/social-insurance reform, following the July omnibus package. Employers and HR/benefits advisers should track the cumulative effect of these successive implementing measures rather than any single decision in isolation.

3. Cross-Border E-Commerce Tax Compliance

Decision No. 295 (14 Sep) tightens the tax-registration procedure for non-residents selling via internet trade into Azerbaijan — relevant to any client operating a cross-border e-commerce or marketplace business reaching Azerbaijani consumers.

4. Parliament and Court Both Quiet, Cabinet Very Active

The Milli Majlis has now gone over two months without a new law and the Constitutional Court three months without a new decision, while the Cabinet issued 37 instruments in three weeks — almost entirely implementing measures for legislation already on the books (Labour Code, Land Code, Customs Code, Road Traffic Law). This is consistent with an autumn dominated by executive implementation of the July legislative package, ahead of the Majlis's own autumn session and the 2027 budget debate.
