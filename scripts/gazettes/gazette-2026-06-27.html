Administration of the President Presidential Office Decrees & Orders — 25 June 2026

President Ilham Aliyev issued a substantial legislative package on 25 June 2026, comprising six presidential decrees (fərmanlar) and three orders (sərəncamlar). The decrees are clustered around two key reform priorities: the digitalisation of customs and state finance infrastructure, and the restructuring of the mandatory health insurance regime, including the creation of a new agency. The orders focus on military recognition and promotions.

Decrees — Fərmanlar (25 June 2026)

Fərman 25 Jun 2026
State Medical Insurance and Expertise Agency — Establishment
"Dövlət Tibbi Sığorta və Ekspertiza Agentliyi" publik hüquqi şəxsin Nizamnaməsinin təsdiq edilməsi haqqında
Formally establishes the "State Medical Insurance and Expertise Agency" as a public legal entity and approves its founding charter (nizamnamə). Centralises oversight of mandatory medical insurance administration under a single new state body, replacing the previous fragmented institutional arrangement.
Fərman 25 Jun 2026
Mandatory Medical Insurance — Amendments to Presidential Decrees
Əhalinin icbari tibbi sığortası sahəsində idarəetmənin təkmilləşdirilməsi ilə bağlı bir sıra tədbirlər haqqında Fərmanın icrası ilə əlaqədar bəzi fərmanlarda dəyişiklik edilməsi barədə
Consequential amendments to a number of existing presidential decrees arising from reforms to mandatory medical insurance governance. Aligns earlier instruments with the newly established agency structure.
Fərman 25 Jun 2026
"Intellectual Customs" Information System — Creation & Regulation
"İntellektual gömrük" informasiya sisteminin yaradılması və bununla əlaqədar bir sıra məsələlərin tənzimlənməsi haqqında
Establishes the "Intellectual Customs" (İntellektual Gömrük) information system and adopts its founding charter. Introduces AI-assisted customs processing and digital risk profiling. Practitioners advising on cross-border trade, customs compliance, and logistics should note this as a significant procedural reform.
Fərman 25 Jun 2026
Reorganisation of State Public Legal Entities
Dövlət adından yaradılan bir sıra publik hüquqi şəxslərin fəaliyyətinin yenidən təşkili ilə bağlı tədbirlər haqqında
Orders the reorganisation of a number of state-created public legal entities. This institutional consolidation reform may affect entities operating under the aegis of specific ministries. Full details of which entities are affected will be published in the official gazette.
Fərman 25 Jun 2026
"Digital State Finance" System — Amendment to Charter
Azərbaycan Respublikası Prezidentinin 2026-cı il 26 yanvar tarixli 590 nömrəli Fərmanı ilə təsdiq edilmiş "Rəqəmsal dövlət maliyyəsi" informasiya sistemi haqqında Əsasnamədə dəyişiklik edilməsi barədə
Amends the charter of the "Digital State Finance" information system originally established by Decree No. 590 (26 January 2026). Refines the system's scope, governance structure, or operating procedures in light of initial implementation experience.
Fərman 25 Jun 2026
Ratification of Protocol Amending 1994 Workplace Accident Investigation Agreement
"Yaşadıqları dövlətdən kənarda olarkən işçilərlə istehsalatda baş vermiş bədbəxt hadisələrin təhqiqatı qaydası haqqında" 1994-cü il 9 dekabr tarixli Sazişə dəyişikliklərin edilməsi haqqında Protokolun təsdiq edilməsi barədə
Approves Azerbaijan's accession to the Protocol amending the 9 December 1994 CIS Agreement on the investigation of workplace accidents occurring outside a worker's home state. Relevant for employers with cross-border operations and labour law practitioners.

Orders — Sərəncamlar (25 June 2026)

Sərəncam 25 Jun 2026
Posthumous Decorations — Military & Civilian Personnel (Territorial Integrity Operations)
Azərbaycan Respublikasının ərazi bütövlüyünün bərpa edilməsi uğrunda gedən döyüş əməliyyatlarında şəhid olmuş hərbi qulluqçularının və mülki işçilərinin təltif edilməsi haqqında
Awards posthumous state decorations to military servicemen and civilian employees who fell during combat operations for the restoration of Azerbaijan's territorial integrity. Issued under Article 109(23) of the Constitution.
Sərəncam 25 Jun 2026
Decorations — Ministry of Defence Military Personnel
Azərbaycan Respublikası Müdafiə Nazirliyinin hərbi qulluqçularının təltif edilməsi haqqında
Confers state decorations on serving members of the Azerbaijani Armed Forces in recognition of distinguished service in maintaining the country's independence and territorial integrity.
Sərəncam 25 Jun 2026
Promotion to General-Major — Defence Ministry Personnel
Azərbaycan Respublikası Müdafiə Nazirliyinin hərbi qulluqçularına "general-mayor" ali hərbi rütbəsinin verilməsi haqqında
Confers the senior military rank of General-Major (general-mayor) on designated Ministry of Defence officers. Issued under Article 109(24) of the Constitution.

New Law Enacted — Qanun (25 June 2026)

Law 25 Jun 2026
Accession to the International Convention on the Control of Harmful Anti-Fouling Systems on Ships (AFS Convention)
"Gəmilərdə zərərli çirklənmə əleyhinə sistemlərə nəzarət haqqında beynəlxalq Konvensiya"ya qoşulmaq barədə Azərbaycan Respublikasının Qanunu
Azerbaijan formally accedes to the IMO's AFS Convention, which bans the use of harmful anti-fouling paints and coatings on ships. Enacted under Article 95(I)(4) of the Constitution. Practitioners advising on maritime law, shipping operations, or environmental compliance should note the new obligations this introduces for vessels flying the Azerbaijani flag.

Institutional Watch

New State Medical Insurance Agency: The creation of the "State Medical Insurance and Expertise Agency" (Dövlət Tibbi Sığorta və Ekspertiza Agentliyi) represents the most significant institutional development of the week. Healthcare providers, insurers, and employers subject to mandatory health insurance obligations should monitor the agency's implementing regulations, which the Cabinet of Ministers is expected to issue in the coming weeks. See also Cabinet Decision No. 170 (9 June 2026) on amendments to the mandatory health insurance services basket.

İqtisadi Kabinet / Nazirlər Kabineti Cabinet of Ministers Decisions & Orders — 20–25 June 2026

The Cabinet of Ministers was highly active during this reporting period, issuing ten Decisions (qərarlar) and four Orders (sərəncamlar) between 22 and 25 June 2026. The week's output spans gas supply reform, business environment improvements, tax monitoring rules, public finance management, NGO funding reform, tourism investment, military service, anti-monopoly implementation, and the extension of the special quarantine regime.

Decisions — Qərarlar

DecisionNo. 199 25 Jun 2026
Automobile Transport — Amendments to Cabinet Decisions
"Avtomobil nəqliyyatı haqqında" Qanununun tətbiqi ilə əlaqədar Prezidentin 539 nömrəli Fərmanının icrası üzrə Nazirlər Kabinetinin bəzi qərarlarında dəyişiklik edilməsi barədə
Consequential amendments to Cabinet decisions implementing Presidential Decree No. 539 (28 November 2025), which applied the Law on Automobile Transport (Law No. 280-VIIQD, 31 October 2025). Aligns subsidiary regulations with the new statutory framework for road freight and passenger transport.
DecisionNo. 198 25 Jun 2026
Horizontal Tax Monitoring — Internal Control & Risk Reporting Rules
"Üfüqi monitorinqə qəbul edilən vergi ödəyiciləri tərəfindən daxili nəzarət sisteminin qurulması və mümkün vergi riskləri barədə məlumatın, həmin risklərə nəzarət prosedurunun vergi orqanına təqdim edilməsi Qaydaları"nın təsdiq edilməsi haqqında
Approves detailed rules governing the internal control systems that taxpayers admitted to the horizontal monitoring programme must establish, along with the format and procedure for disclosing potential tax risks to the tax authority. Tax practitioners advising large corporate clients should closely analyse the compliance obligations established by this Decision.
DecisionNo. 196 25 Jun 2026
SME State Support — Assessment Rules
"Dövlət orqanları (qurumları) tərəfindən mikro, kiçik və orta sahibkarlıq subyektlərinə … dəstək göstərilməsinin qiymətləndirilməsi Qaydaları"nın təsdiq edilməsi haqqında
Adopts rules for assessing the effectiveness of state support provided to micro, small and medium-sized enterprises (MSMEs) by state bodies, state-funded institutions, and MSME support infrastructure bodies. Introduces a performance measurement framework for state-backed MSME programmes.
DecisionNo. 195 24 Jun 2026
Special Quarantine Regime — Extension
Azərbaycan Respublikasının ərazisində xüsusi karantin rejiminin müddətinin uzadılması haqqında
Extends the duration of the special quarantine regime in force on the territory of Azerbaijan. Practitioners and clients in sectors subject to quarantine-related restrictions should verify the new end date and any updated operational requirements.
DecisionNo. 192 23 Jun 2026
Anti-Monopoly Reform — Amendments to Cabinet Decisions
"Antiinhisar və istehlak bazarına nəzarət sahələrində idarəetmənin təkmilləşdirilməsi" üzrə Prezidentin 520 nömrəli Fərmanının icrası ilə əlaqədar Nazirlər Kabinetinin bəzi qərarlarında dəyişiklik edilməsi haqqında
Amends and repeals certain Cabinet decisions in implementation of Presidential Decree No. 520 (6 November 2025) on improving the governance of anti-monopoly regulation and consumer market oversight. Part of the broader reform of Azerbaijan's competition law enforcement architecture.
DecisionNo. 191 23 Jun 2026

Nazirlər Kabinetinin 2015-ci il 2 fevral tarixli 21 nömrəli Qərarı ilə təsdiq edilmiş "Bağlaşma əsasında həqiqi hərbi xidmətə daxilolmanın halları və Qaydası"nda dəyişiklik edilməsi haqqında
Amends the rules on the conditions and procedures for admission to active military service on a contract basis, originally established by Cabinet Decision No. 21 (2 February 2015). Adjusts eligibility criteria or administrative procedures for contract military recruitment.
DecisionNo. 189 23 Jun 2026

"Qaz təchizatı sisteminə dair Qaydalar"ın təsdiq edilməsi və Nazirlər Kabinetinin 2023-cü il 30 avqust tarixli 297 nömrəli Qərarının ləğv edilməsi barədə
Adopts comprehensive new Gas Supply System Rules, replacing and revoking Cabinet Decision No. 297 (30 August 2023). This is a significant regulatory reset for the gas sector, consolidating rules on gas distribution, connection procedures, quality standards, and supplier obligations into a single new regulatory instrument. Energy law practitioners should treat this as priority reading.
DecisionNo. 190 22 Jun 2026

Regulatory Spotlight — Key Themes, Week 26 / 2026

1. Mandatory Health Insurance Overhaul

The creation of the State Medical Insurance and Expertise Agency, together with consequential amendments across multiple presidential decrees, signals a structural reform of how mandatory health insurance is administered in Azerbaijan. The new agency absorbs functions that were previously distributed across several bodies. Employers, healthcare providers, and private insurers should prepare for updated compliance obligations as the agency's implementing regulations emerge. Monitor for Cabinet decisions from the new entity in Q3 2026.

2. Digital Customs & State Finance Infrastructure

Two of this week's presidential decrees — the "Intellectual Customs" system and the amendment to the "Digital State Finance" charter — represent continued investment in Azerbaijan's digital governance architecture. The AI-assisted customs system in particular has direct implications for import/export clearance timelines, risk-based inspections, and trade documentation. Logistics, customs, and international trade practitioners should note this as a significant procedural shift for cross-border transactions.

3. Gas Supply Reform — Full Regulatory Reset

Cabinet Decision No. 189 replaces the gas distribution rulebook. The revocation of the 2023 framework and adoption of new comprehensive Rules signals a deliberate consolidation effort. Agreements referencing the previous regulatory regime may require review. This is consistent with the broader pattern of gas sector reform since the enactment of the Law on Gas Supply (Law No. 233-VIIQ, 8 July 2025). Legal teams advising energy clients should flag the need for a regulatory impact assessment.

4. Export Diversification & SME Finance

Three instruments this week form a coherent policy cluster on economic diversification: Order No. 436s (credit subsidies for entrepreneurs), Order No. 424s (non-oil export stimulation and transport cost support), and Decision No. 196 (SME support effectiveness assessment). Together these implement a suite of presidential decrees from 9 June 2026 (Decrees 680 and 681). Firms advising SME clients or export-oriented businesses should flag the availability of new subsidy instruments and assess their clients' eligibility under the new frameworks once implementing rules are published.
