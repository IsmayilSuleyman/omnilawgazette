Office of the President Presidential Decrees Fərmanlar — 10–14 August 2026

The President signed four decrees during the reporting period, spanning cybersecurity governance, agricultural regulation, statistical cooperation with Belarus, and public-finance oversight infrastructure.

Decree 10 Aug 2026
Implementation of Amendments to the Law "On Information, Informatization and Protection of Information"
Establishes Computer Emergency Response Teams (CERTs) for information infrastructure supporting public functions, and directs the Cabinet of Ministers to draft rules for the cybersecurity of state-body and strategic-facility information systems, plus a regulatory sandbox framework for testing new security measures.
Source: president.az, via APA.az / Trend.az reporting — signing date not independently re-verified on the primary site this pass.
Decree 13 Aug 2026
Implementation of the Law "On Seed Production"
Directs the Cabinet of Ministers to establish state-control procedures and a seed and breeding-stock registry within five months, operationalizing the newly adopted Seed Production Law.
Source: president.az, via APA.az / Trend.az reporting — signing date not independently re-verified on the primary site this pass.
Decree 14 Aug 2026
Approval of the Azerbaijan–Belarus Memorandum of Understanding on Statistical Cooperation
Approves the MoU signed in Minsk on 2 June 2026 and directs the relevant state bodies to take implementation steps for joint statistical work with Belarus.
Source: president.az, via APA.az / Trend.az reporting — signing date not independently re-verified on the primary site this pass.
Decree 14 Aug 2026
Establishment of the "State Audit" Information System
Creates the "State Audit" information system and approves its governing regulation, tasking the Chamber of Accounts with its development — a step toward digitizing public-finance oversight.
Source: president.az, via APA.az / Trend.az reporting — signing date not independently re-verified on the primary site this pass.

Regulatory Spotlight — Practitioner Notes

Cybersecurity: CERTs Become Mandatory Infrastructure

The 10 August decree operationalizes the CERT requirement flagged in earlier information-security reforms. Clients operating information systems that support "public functions" — a category likely to be defined broadly by the Cabinet's forthcoming implementing rules — should start scoping what a CERT stand-up would require, and watch for the regulatory sandbox framework, which may offer a lower-friction path to pilot new security tooling ahead of full compliance deadlines.

Public Finance: "State Audit" System Signals Digitization Push

Pairing the new State Audit information system with the Chamber of Accounts' existing oversight mandate suggests a move toward continuous, data-driven audit rather than periodic review. Entities that receive state funding or operate under state audit jurisdiction should expect increased data-reporting requests as the system comes online.
