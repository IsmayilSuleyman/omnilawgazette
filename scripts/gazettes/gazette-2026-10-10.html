Nazirlər Kabineti Cabinet of Ministers Decisions & Orders — 5 – 9 October 2026

The Cabinet issued ten Decisions (Nos. 321–330) and four Orders (Nos. 740s, 744s, 747s, 749s) between 5 and 9 October. Most implement recent Presidential Decrees (Nos. 792, 794, 797 and others).

Decisions (Qərarlar)

Embassy, Mission & Consulate Regulations — Consequential Amendments
Amends several Cabinet decisions in implementation of Presidential Decree No. 638 of 3 April 2026, which approved the new Regulations on Embassies, Permanent Missions, Consulates and Honorary Consuls.
DecisionNo. 329 9 Oct 2026
Foreign Trade Commodity Codes (Cabinet-Order Goods List) — Amendment
Amends the HS/FEA commodity-nomenclature codes in the List of goods whose export and import is carried out by Cabinet order, approved by Cabinet Decision No. 50 of 25 February 2025.

DecisionNo. 324 6 Oct 2026

Amends and repeals certain Cabinet decisions in implementation of Presidential Decree No. 599 of 20 February 2026 “On improving the activity of a number of state bodies”.

Model Regulation on Vehicle Parking Areas & Garages — Amendment

DecisionNo. 322 6 Oct 2026
Utility-Cost Support for Entrepreneurs in Liberated Territories — Amendment
Amends Cabinet Decision No. 180 of 2 June 2023 on the procedure for paying financial assistance for utilities (electricity, gas, water supply, wastewater) to entrepreneurs engaged in production in the liberated territories.

Amends Cabinet Decision No. 296 of 11 August 2022 on the content of written instructions restricting or halting works endangering monuments in Shusha city, and the related procedure and timelines.

Regulatory Spotlight — Key Themes, 3 – 9 Oct 2026

1. Parliament’s Autumn Agenda Takes Shape

With the autumn work plan adopted on 30 September, the bills register now holds a draft Civil Service Code, a Criminal Code / Criminal Procedure Code amendment, Labour Code and aviation amendments, and changes to the laws on public procurement and technical regulation. Public-sector employment, procurement and criminal-procedure practitioners should monitor these drafts.

2. State-Asset Consolidation

Orders Nos. 747s and 740s and No. 749s implement decrees reshaping the portfolios of the Azerbaijan Investment Holding and AZCON, and the reorganisation of Baku Metropoliteni CJSC — relevant to M&A, state-contract and corporate-restructuring work.

3. Product & Education Regulation

Decision No. 327 approves Technical Regulation TR 002/2026/AZ on toy safety (importers, retailers); Decision No. 325 sets attire requirements in state general schools; Order No. 744s activates the 2027–2030 mining and metallurgy State Program.

4. Social & Trade Housekeeping

Decision No. 328 amends the labour-pension rules and No. 329 updates commodity codes in the Cabinet-order goods list; No. 330 aligns Cabinet decisions with the new regulations on embassies, missions and consulates (Decree No. 638).
