Headline: The week belongs to the executive. With the Milli Majlis in summer recess and the Constitutional Court between plenary terms, the Presidential Administration promulgated the remaining July land-and-cadastre package on 2 September, together with the application decrees and the long-awaited replacement of the 2004 Rules on the State Register of Immovable Property. The Cabinet, for its part, released a block of ten implementation orders that start four- and five-month clocks for secondary legislation on heat supply, seed production, fisheries-adjacent permits, state audit, SME governance and customs.

Administration of the President Laws Promulgated president.az › Sənədlər › Qanunlar — 27 August & 2 September 2026

All five laws below were adopted by the Milli Majlis on 14 July 2026 and signed by the President on the same date; they entered the public record when the Presidential Administration published them this week. Unless a law states otherwise, it enters into force on the day of its official publication.

LawNo. 457-VIIQ · 14 Jul 2026 Published 27 Aug 2026
Law of the Republic of Azerbaijan “On Fisheries” (Balıqçılıq haqqında)
A new framework law replacing the 1998 regime. It defines fishing, aquaculture, fishing quotas, fishing vessels (which must carry tracking equipment), fish-breeding and acclimatisation, and it regulates commercial, sport and amateur fishing in “fishery water bodies”. Aquaculture farms become registrable entities. Article 17 gives the state fisheries inspectorate the right to access data from technical control devices installed by operators and to build monitoring systems; Article 18 subjects facilities that may affect fishery water bodies to state ecological expertise. The regulator is designated by the companion decree (see below) as the Ministry of Agriculture.
Law14 Jul 2026 (companion act) Published 27 Aug 2026
Amendments to the Laws “On State Duty” and “On Licences and Permits”
Two-article law tied to the Fisheries Law. Article 18.69 of the State Duty Law is restated to set the fee for issuing an aquaculture farm registration certificate at AZN 70. Annex 3 to the Licences and Permits Law (the list of permits for entrepreneurial activity) is amended at item 16 to add the aquaculture registration certificate as a permit.
LawNo. 462-VIIQD · 14 Jul 2026 Published 2 Sep 2026
Amendments to the Code of Administrative Offences
A multi-topic amendment. New Article 37-1 allows a prosecutor or authorised official to order a psychiatric examination, without the person's consent, where delay would endanger the person or others; involuntary placement follows the Law on Psychiatric Care and the Civil Procedure Code. Article 38.1 is extended to the cybersecurity of information infrastructure and a new Article 371-2 creates cybersecurity offences (jurisdiction: National Cybersecurity Agency). Article 287 (seed production) is rewritten. A new Article 558.1-1 penalises officials who fail to respond in time to queries from the immovable-property registry information system, with a warning or a fine of AZN 250–400. Entry into force: on publication, except items 1.2, 1.3 and 1.5 (cybersecurity) from 1 July 2027 and item 1.9 from 1 January 2027.
LawNo. 460-VIIQD · 14 Jul 2026 Published 2 Sep 2026
Amendments to the Land Code, the Civil Code and the Laws on the State Cadastre of Immovable Property, on the Land Market and on the State Register of Immovable Property
Introduces Article 13-2 of the Land Code — consolidation of agricultural land. Consolidation is defined as the merger, division, re-delimitation or exchange of agricultural plots, with the owners' consent and on the basis of equivalent market value, followed by re-registration of ownership and other rights in rem and, where needed, adjustment of irrigation, road, energy and other infrastructure. Value is fixed by a licensed valuer under the Law on Valuation Activity; an increase in value creates no payment obligation for the owner, while a shortfall is compensated under a procedure to be set by the Cabinet (Art. 13-2.6). The State Register Law gains a new registrable document: the consolidation distribution document (Art. 8.0.13).
LawNo. 461-VIIQD · 14 Jul 2026 Published 2 Sep 2026
Amendments to the Land Code, the Civil Code, the Laws on Privatisation of the Housing Stock, on the State Land Cadastre, on the State Register of Immovable Property, Law No. 918-VQD of 2017 and the Law on Agrarian Insurance
The terminology shift from “state land cadastre” to “state cadastre of immovable property” is carried through seven acts. Article 41 of the Land Code is restated: the cadastre covers all immovable property within the country regardless of form of ownership and is maintained by the designated body through its information system (designated by decree as the State Service for Property Issues and its Electronic Registry and Cadastre system). Article 4 of Law No. 918-VQD (2017) is repealed and Article 13.4.3 of the Agrarian Insurance Law is conformed.
Administration of the President Decrees & Orders president.az › Fərmanlar / Sərəncamlar — 27 August & 2 September 2026

Seven decrees and three orders were signed in the period. Serial numbers for the 2 September instruments had not yet been assigned in the online record at the time of compilation; they will appear in the Qanunvericilik Toplusu. Deadlines noted below run from the signing date.

Decrees (Fərmanlar)

DecreeApplication of Law 457-VIIQ 27 Aug 2026
Application of the Law “On Fisheries”; amendment and repeal of related decrees and orders
Allocates the “relevant executive authority” powers in the Fisheries Law among the President, the Cabinet of Ministers and, for the bulk of operational functions (quotas, fishing rules, aquaculture registration, monitoring), the Ministry of Agriculture. Repeals Decree No. 722 of 1998 (and later amendments) on the former regime. The Foreign Ministry is instructed to notify the Swiss Government, as CITES depositary, that the Ministry of Agriculture is the management and scientific authority for fish and aquatic bio-resources and the Ministry of Science and Education the scientific authority for other wild fauna and flora.
DecreeApplication of Law 462-VIIQD 2 Sep 2026
Application of the Administrative Offences Code amendments; amendment of Decree No. 795 (2016)
Names the competent bodies: the Ministry of Internal Affairs for psychiatric-examination decisions (Art. 37-1.1); the Ministry of Agriculture's “Electronic Agriculture” seed sub-system (Art. 287); the Ministry of Digital Development and Transport, acting as National Cybersecurity Agency, for Art. 371-2 offences, with the State Security Service and the Special Communication and Information Security Service for the note to that article; and the Ministry of Economy's Electronic Registry and Cadastre system for Art. 558.1-1. The cybersecurity designations take effect on 1 July 2027.
DecreeApplication of Law 460-VIIQD 2 Sep 2026
Application of the land-consolidation amendments; amendment of Decree No. 176 (1999)
The President exercises the powers in Land Code Art. 13-2.5 and the Cabinet those in Art. 13-2.6. The Cabinet must adopt the agricultural land consolidation procedure within six months (by early March 2027) and report to the President. The State Water Resources Agency is added to the bodies named in the 1999 application decree.
DecreeApplication of Law 461-VIIQD 2 Sep 2026
Application of the cadastre amendments; amendment of several decrees and repeal of Decree No. 135 (2004)
Designates the State Service for Property Issues under the Ministry of Economy as the body maintaining the state cadastre and register of immovable property through its Electronic Registry and Cadastre information system. Technical inventory is redefined as “field measurement work”. Decree No. 135 of 14 October 2004, which approved the Rules on Maintaining the State Register of Immovable Property, is repealed; two new instruments were published the same day: the Rules on maintaining the state register of immovable property, including the composition of data held in the register and the Rules on maintaining the state cadastre of immovable property (both available as PDFs on president.az).
DecreeAmending Decree No. 1361 (2017) 2 Sep 2026
List of officials authorised to draw up protocols on administrative offences heard by district (city) and military courts
Adds part 41-1: protocols under Art. 428.9 may be drawn up by the Ministry of Internal Affairs or the State Agency for Antimonopoly and Consumer Market Control, whichever detected the offence. Part 68 is extended to cover new Art. 558.1-1 (registry-query offences).
DecreeAmending Decree No. 1573 (2017) 2 Sep 2026
Statute of the “Icherisheher” State Historical-Architectural Reserve Administration
Implements Decree No. 629 of 19 March 2026 on the pay system of publicly funded public legal entities: the Administration's board gains power to set an incentive-pay system for staff (cl. 4.5.7) and clause 5.7 is repealed.
DecreeInternational instrument 2 Sep 2026
Approval of the Memorandum of Understanding on statistical cooperation between the State Statistics Committee of Azerbaijan and the National Statistics Committee of the Kyrgyz Republic
Signed in Cholpon-Ata on 31 July 2026. The State Statistics Committee will implement it once in force; the Foreign Ministry is to notify Bishkek of completion of domestic procedures.

Orders (Sərəncamlar)

OrderConscription 2 Sep 2026
Call-up of citizens to active military service from 1 to 30 October 2026 and discharge of conscripts to the reserve
Citizens born in 2008 who have reached 18 by the call-up date, and those born 1996–2007 under the age of 30 who have not served and have no deferment or exemption, are called up between 1 and 30 October 2026. Conscripts who have completed the statutory term under Art. 38.1.1 of the Law on Military Duty and Military Service are discharged in the same window. HR teams should expect the usual employment-suspension requests under the Labour Code.
OrderAmending Order No. 3910 (2023) 2 Sep 2026
State Programme on the Socio-Economic Development of the Nakhchivan Autonomous Republic 2023–2027
Substantive rewrite of several priority directions. Para 4.1.2 now promises targeted logistics-support mechanisms for Nakhchivan producers to cut transport costs and reach domestic and foreign markets, and states that once the Zangezur corridor opens, the introduction of incentives for overland exports from Nakhchivan will be separately assessed. Organic-product branding language is dropped, afforestation measures are broadened to general greening, and AZAL is removed from the list of executing bodies.
OrderReserve fund allocation 27 Aug 2026
Financial support to “Sabah” football club
Allocates AZN 5.0 million from the President's reserve fund in the 2026 state budget in connection with the club's qualification for the league phase of the UEFA Champions League. The Ministry of Finance funds; the Cabinet resolves consequential matters.

On the petition of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, the Islamic Republic of Iran is added as item 8 to Annex 6, the list of foreign states in a state of war or armed conflict where Azerbaijani missions and consulates operate, triggering the enhanced hardship compensation regime for posted staff. Orders — programme implementation

OrderNo. 639s 28 Aug 2026
Implementation of Presidential Order No. 1182 of 20 August 2026 approving the State Programme for Tourism Development 2026–2030
Sets the first deliverables under the new programme. The State Tourism Agency must submit proposals to improve the Law “On Tourism” by 20 December 2026, and by 20 October 2026 proposals on issuing investment-promotion documents for tourism projects and on a flexible incentive framework (programme para 8.8.2). By the same October date the Ministry of Economy must propose interest subsidies on manat loans for hotels, sanatoria, winter-sports arenas and congress venues in tourism and recreation zones, and the Ministry of Digital Development and Transport must propose how aviation can contribute more to tourism. The Centre for Analysis of Economic Reforms and Communication builds a monitoring module on the e-monitoring portal within two months; monitoring runs from 2027 with annual reports by 30 April and a final evaluation by 30 June 2031. Executing bodies file quarterly project data from 2027.

Regulatory Spotlight — Key Themes, Week 36 / 2026

1. The immovable-property cadastre is rebuilt

Laws 460 and 461-VIIQD, their application decrees and the two new sets of Rules published on 2 September replace the 2004 registry rules with a single “state cadastre of immovable property” run by the State Service for Property Issues through its electronic registry. Real-estate, project-finance and title-due-diligence teams should re-check document names, data composition and query procedures against the new Rules, and note the new administrative liability for officials who ignore registry queries (Art. 558.1-1).

2. Agricultural land consolidation arrives

Article 13-2 of the Land Code makes voluntary, valuation-based consolidation of farmland a recognised legal process with its own registrable distribution document. The Cabinet has six months to write the procedure and compensation mechanism. Agribusiness clients holding fragmented plots, and lenders secured on such plots, should watch the draft procedure and consider how mortgages and leases survive re-delimitation.

3. Fisheries and aquaculture get a modern statute

The new Fisheries Law, its application decree and the AZN 70 registration certificate create a permit-based aquaculture sector under the Ministry of Agriculture, with vessel tracking, quota control and mandatory ecological expertise for facilities affecting fishery waters. Existing operators need to map their activities to the new registration and permit regime; the Cabinet's 27 August orders indicate that implementing regulations are due within five months.

4. Cybersecurity enforcement from July 2027

New Article 371-2 of the Administrative Offences Code, enforced by the National Cybersecurity Agency within the Ministry of Digital Development and Transport, takes effect on 1 July 2027. Operators of information infrastructure have ten months to align internal controls; the companion primary rules should be expected in the autumn session.

5. Tourism programme: first deadlines in October

Order 639s turns the 2026–2030 State Programme into concrete proposals due by 20 October (investment-promotion documents, incentive framework, interest subsidies for hotels and resorts, aviation) and 20 December (revision of the Law on Tourism). Hospitality and infrastructure investors have a window to feed proposals to the State Tourism Agency and the Ministry of Economy.

6. Iran designated a conflict-zone posting

Cabinet Decision 261 adds Iran to the list of war or armed-conflict states for diplomatic compensation purposes, retroactive to 1 March 2026 and expiring 31 December 2026. Beyond its HR effect for the Foreign Ministry, it is an official signal relevant to force-majeure, insurance and travel-risk assessments for clients with Iranian exposure.
