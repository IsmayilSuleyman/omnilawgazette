Parliament of Azerbaijan Milli Majlis Summer Recess — no plenary activity
On Recess. The Milli Majlis (National Assembly) is in its statutory summer recess, which runs from 15 July to 30 August 2026. Deputies held their final extraordinary-session sitting on 14 July 2026; no laws, decrees, bills or reports have been registered since. No plenary business is expected to resume before the end of August, absent an extraordinary session being called. All of the Assembly's four public registers (Laws, Decrees, Reports, Bills) show their most recent entries dated 10–14 July 2026.
İqtisadi Kabinet / Nazirlər Kabineti Cabinet of Ministers Decisions & Orders — 3–4 August 2026

With Parliament out of session, the Cabinet of Ministers was the most active domestic legislative body this week, issuing six Decisions and six Orders, concentrated in a single working session on 3–4 August. Several Orders formalise implementation of laws and strategies adopted earlier in the year.

Administration of the President Presidential Office Decrees & Orders — 3–7 August 2026

The President signed twelve decrees (fərmanlar) and thirteen orders (sərəncamlar) this week, with activity concentrated on 6–7 August. Two threads dominate: implementation of recent Customs Code, media-legislation and Public Procurement changes, and a governance overhaul of state transport/space enterprises, alongside a cluster of ambassadorial reassignments. A single diplomatic letter (to Swiss President Guy Parmelin, marking Switzerland's National Day) was recorded on 1 August — a much quieter week for correspondence than Issue No. 1's National Salvation Day cluster.

Regulatory Spotlight — Key Themes, Week 32 / 2026

1. State Transport & Space Enterprise Governance Overhaul

The 6 August decree restructuring Azerkosmos, AZCON, Azerbaijan Airlines and Caspian Shipping is the week's most structurally significant instrument — new Supervisory and Management Boards, and a shift of Airlines' general-assembly powers to the President/AZCON. Corporate teams advising these entities or their counterparties should review governance and signing-authority implications once the full decree text is available.

2. Media & Broadcasting Law Implementation

Four presidential decrees on 7 August operationalise the July 2026 state-service/media law amendments, including the new structure of the Media and Broadcasting Council and revised media-registry rules. Media, telecoms and advertising clients should watch for registry-compliance deadlines in the implementing text.

3. Competition & Fintech Rulemaking

Cabinet Decision No. 258 sets the operative merger-control notification thresholds for Azerbaijan's Competition Authority, while Order No. 599s pushes forward crowdfunding-law implementation. Both merit a first read once gazetted — potential impact on deal timing (M&A) and on fintech platform licensing.

4. Parliament in Recess — Executive Fills the Gap

With the Milli Majlis on summer recess through 30 August, this week's legislative activity is entirely executive: Cabinet decisions/orders and presidential decrees/orders implementing laws passed before recess (Customs Code, Tax Code, June's administrative-offences/data-protection/child-safety omnibus, and the media law package). Expect a burst of new bills once Parliament reconvenes.
