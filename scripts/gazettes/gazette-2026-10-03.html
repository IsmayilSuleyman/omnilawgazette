Related instrument just before the window — Decree No. 793 (24 Sep 2026): per-capita financing in general schools. From the 2026–2027 academic year, state general-education institutions move in phases to per-capita financing, with growth coefficients by education level. The Cabinet must approve per-student cost amounts and financing rules within one month (i.e. by about 24 October 2026); the Ministry of Science and Education sets the annual list of institutions and student numbers with the Ministry of Finance. Cabinet Order No. 728 (2 Oct) is the first implementing step.

Regulatory Spotlight — Key Themes, 26 Sep – 3 Oct 2026

1. Civil Service Code Tabled

A new Civil Service Code (bill 2702) comes with two companion personnel packages (2696, 2698) amending tax, military, police and customs service provisions. Employment and public-sector advisers should track the committee stage.

2. Procurement, Tax & Inspections Reform Cluster

Bills 2693 (public procurement), 2697 (Tax Code, Migration Code, social insurance) and 2688 (inspections suspension, EIA) form a business-regulation cluster likely to affect contractors and foreign employers.

3. Kimberley Process Accession

The 29 Sep Decree builds the customs framework for rough diamonds ahead of accession to the Kimberley Process Certification Scheme; effective on the accession date.

4. School Financing Overhaul

Decree 793 and Cabinet Order 728 start a shift to per-capita funding for state schools; the Cabinet’s one-month deadline falls in late October.
