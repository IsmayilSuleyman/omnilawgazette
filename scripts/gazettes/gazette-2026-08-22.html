Parliament of Azerbaijan Milli Majlis Summer Recess — no new activity

The Milli Majlis remained in its customary summer recess throughout the reporting period; no plenary session convened and no laws or decrees were enacted between 16–22 August 2026. The chamber's most recent activity was an extraordinary session that concluded on 14 July 2026, at which it adopted a large omnibus package — Laws No. 446–472-VIIQD/VIIQ — spanning gas supply, road traffic, the Tax Code and food safety, enforcement procedure, aviation, energy efficiency, the Migration Code, mandatory medical insurance, the Land and Civil Codes, plant breeding, crowdfunding, civil service and media law, procurement, and customs. This week's wave of Presidential decrees and Cabinet implementation orders (see below) is the executive branch putting that package into effect.

Note on Bills: The bills-under-discussion register (cat-layihe.php?cat=83) shows no items newer than 14 July 2026. No new bills were placed on the agenda during 16–22 August 2026.
İqtisadi Kabinet / Nazirlər Kabineti Cabinet of Ministers Decisions & Orders — 17–19 August 2026

The Cabinet issued two Decisions on personnel remuneration and, in a single block on 17 August, fifteen Orders (Nos. 603s–617s) each directing agencies to implement a specific Presidential decree that itself gives effect to the 14 July omnibus law. No items were dated later than 19 August at the time of compilation.

Regulatory Spotlight — Key Themes, Week 34 / 2026

1. The Implementation Cascade

This week's volume — 11 decrees, 5 presidential orders, and 15 Cabinet implementation orders — is almost entirely the executive branch operationalising the Milli Majlis's 14 July omnibus package (Laws No. 446–472). Clients with matters touching the Customs Code, Tax Code, Administrative Offenses Code, Land/Civil Codes, or transport and energy-efficiency law should expect implementing regulations to keep surfacing through September.

2. Azerbaijan–Turkmenistan Cooperation Package

Five decrees on 21 August approve agreements on food security, customs statistics, energy, defence, and broader economic cooperation with Turkmenistan (signed 22 June 2026 in Baku). This is the most significant bilateral development of the week — worth monitoring for clients in energy, logistics, and cross-border trade.

3. New SME, Investment & Export Promotion Agency

The 20 August decrees establishing a dedicated Agency under the Ministry of Economy consolidate SME support, investment promotion, and export promotion functions. Corporate advisory teams should track the agency's forthcoming regulations, as it will likely become a key counterparty for incentive and licensing matters.

4. Land Code & Valuation Law Now Live

The 17 August decree implementing the Land Code, Civil Code, and Valuation Activities Law amendments brings the 14 July land-reform package into force. Real estate and land-transaction practice groups should review the amended provisions immediately, as they may affect pending deals.
