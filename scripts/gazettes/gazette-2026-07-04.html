Regulatory Spotlight — Key Themes, Week 27 / 2026

1. Untracked Presidential Decrees on Investment & Enforcement

Cabinet Orders 453s and 454s implement Presidential Decrees No. 686 and No. 689 — on administrative-violation enforcement authority and investment/tax incentives, respectively — neither of which surfaced in the public English decree feed this week. Firms advising on investment structuring or regulatory enforcement exposure should request the full decree texts directly rather than relying on secondary references.

2. Large Financial-Regulation Package Incoming

The Majlis's agenda for its 7 July session lists eleven bills touching crowdfunding, the Tax Code, the Customs Code, banking, securities, and the Audit Chamber in a single coordinated package. This is the most significant forward-looking signal of the week for financial-services and tax practices — worth pre-briefing clients ahead of first readings.

3. Education & Culture Financing Reforms

Decisions 202–204 collectively touch vocational and professional education funding and specialties classification, alongside a new grant-competition procedure for culture-sector financing. Institutions receiving state education or culture funding should review per-student cost formulas and new competition eligibility criteria once gazetted.

4. Cross-Code Enforcement Implementation

Decision No. 200 implements recent amendments spanning the Labor Code, social protection rules, family law, and criminal enforcement in a single instrument — a sign of coordinated rollout across previously separate legislative amendments. Compliance and employment teams should map which of their existing obligations are affected once full text is available on e-qanun.az.
