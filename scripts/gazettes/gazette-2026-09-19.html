İqtisadi Kabinet / Nazirlər Kabineti Cabinet of Ministers Decisions & Orders — 12–18 September 2026

The Cabinet of Ministers issued nine Decisions and no Orders during the reporting period, clustered in two batches: 14 September (Nos. 295–296) and 16–17 September (Nos. 297–303).

Decisions (Qərarlar) — I

DecisionNo. 295 14 Sep 2026
Non-Resident E-Commerce VAT Procedure — Amendment
Amends the Rules (approved by Decision No. 387, 30 October 2023) on the electronic tax registration, re-registration and de-registration, VAT-payer registration, VAT return filing and VAT payment of non-residents conducting e-commerce through internet information resources.
DecisionNo. 296 14 Sep 2026
Veterinary Normative Acts — Amendment
Amends Decision No. 66 (16 April 2007) approving certain normative legal acts related to veterinary matters.
DecisionNo. 297 16 Sep 2026
Culture Ministry Subordinate Institutions List — Amendment
Amends the list of institutions subordinate to, but outside the structure of, the Ministry of Culture (approved by Decision No. 96).
DecisionNo. 298 16 Sep 2026
Qarayazı State Nature Reserve — Sanitation-Protection Zone
Approves the sanitation-protection zone of the Qarayazı State Nature Reserve.

Decisions (Qərarlar) — II

DecisionNo. 299 16 Sep 2026
Social Payments Procedure — Amendment
Amends the Rules (approved by Decision No. 368, 30 July 2024) on assigning, paying, suspending payment of, and safeguarding case files for, social payments.
DecisionNo. 300 16 Sep 2026
Road Traffic Law — Implementing Amendments
Amends certain Cabinet decisions in connection with the entry into force of the Law "On Road Traffic" (Law No. 379-VIIQD, 17 March 2026).
DecisionNo. 301 17 Sep 2026
Strategic Objects Protection Agency — Staff Health Protection
Regulates a number of matters concerning the protection of the health of employees of the State Agency for the Protection of Strategic Objects, and repeals the prior Decision No. 54 (29 January 2024) on the same subject.
DecisionNo. 302 17 Sep 2026
Baku Land Plot Designated for Renewable Energy
Designates a state-owned land plot in the administrative territory of Baku city as a renewable-energy-source zone.
DecisionNo. 303 17 Sep 2026
Special Quarantine Regime — Extension
Extends the duration of the special quarantine regime in the territory of the Republic of Azerbaijan.

Regulatory Spotlight — Key Themes, 12–18 Sep 2026

1. Special Quarantine Regime Extended

Decision No. 303 extends the duration of the special quarantine regime in the territory of the Republic of Azerbaijan. Businesses with quarantine-sensitive operations (cross-border logistics, agriculture, veterinary trade) should confirm the new end date and any updated conditions directly with the Cabinet's published text.

2. Baku Land Earmarked for Renewable Energy

Decision No. 302 designates a state-owned land plot within Baku's administrative territory as a renewable-energy-source zone, continuing the government's steady allocation of land for solar/wind development. Energy-sector clients evaluating siting or land-use opportunities should track the parcel's exact boundaries once published.

3. Non-Resident E-Commerce VAT Rules Refined

Decision No. 295 amends the 2023 procedure governing tax registration, VAT-payer registration, return filing and payment for non-resident entities selling via e-commerce into Azerbaijan. Clients advising foreign online sellers or marketplaces should review the amendment for any change to registration thresholds or filing mechanics.

4. Quiet Week for New Legislation

No new laws, bills, or parliamentary decrees were recorded this period, and the Cabinet issued decisions only — no Orders. This continues the pattern since the Milli Majlis's summer recess; the last substantive legislative burst was the 14 July omnibus package (Laws 452–472-VIIQD). Firms should expect renewed parliamentary activity once the autumn session opens.
