Regulatory Spotlight — Key Themes, 5 – 11 Sep 2026

1. Land Market & Cadastre Reform Package

Orders No. 673s and 674s implement two Presidential Decrees (Nos. 784, 785) and two Laws (Nos. 460–461-VIIQD) amending the Land Code, Civil Code, and land-market rules, alongside Decision No. 292 on state land-acquisition needs. Together these point to an active land-market and cadastre reform underway — real estate, agricultural, and construction practices should track implementing regulations closely as they land.

2. Busiest Cabinet Week Tracked So Far

31 Decisions and 5 Orders in four days is more than double Issue No. 2's volume, touching everything from customs and healthcare to organ-transplant logistics and Nakhchivan's constitutional amendments (Decision No. 278). The clustering on 8–9 September suggests a backlog of law-implementation decisions was cleared ahead of the parliamentary autumn session.

3. Milli Majlis Committees Stir Ahead of 30 September

Plenary registers (laws, decrees, bills, reports) are still empty, but standing committees resumed work on 11 September to plan their autumn agenda, per independent press reporting. Firms should expect the pace of new legislation to pick up materially from the last week of September.

4. Heritage & Culture Administration Continues

Decision No. 274 and Order No. 657s continue the İçərişəhər (Old City) Reserve's administrative restructuring, while Decision No. 280 transfers cultural venues to direct Ministry of Culture oversight — a continuation of the cultural-heritage administration theme flagged in Issue No. 2.
