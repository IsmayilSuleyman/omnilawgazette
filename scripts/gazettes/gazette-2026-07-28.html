Lead Story The July Package Lands: Ten Statutes Into Force in Six Days Decrees of 23, 24, 27 and 28 July 2026

This was the week the Milli Majlis's closing legislative burst of the spring session became operative law. Between 23 and 28 July the President issued thirteen decrees that applied ten separate statutes — touching the Tax Code, the Labour Code, the Migration Code, the Housing Code, and the laws on aviation, road traffic, execution, medical insurance, psychiatric care and crowdfunding. The Cabinet of Ministers issued a further sixteen instruments in the same six days, most of them the second-order implementing steps for the same package. For a single week outside session, this is an unusually dense volume of operative change.

Three of these decrees do more than switch a statute on — they open regulatory projects that will run into 2027. The Crowdfunding Law hands six categories of rulemaking to the Central Bank. The Aviation Law amendments require the Cabinet to build, from nothing, a regulatory regime for ultralight aircraft. And the Tax Code decree creates a new VAT-deposit transfer mechanism that commences on 1 January 2027.

Flagship — Law No. 450-VIIQ "On Crowdfunding"

The decree of 24 July brings into operation the Republic's first dedicated statutory framework for investment- and lending-based crowdfunding. The President reserves the powers arising under Articles 3.2 and 18.8. The Cabinet has four months to align presidential acts and three months to designate the offshore zones caught by the Law's restrictions. The Central Bank has six months to fix the parameters that will decide whether the market is commercially workable: operator capital and minimum aggregate capital thresholds; platform technical standards; per-project and per-platform investment ceilings for individual investors; the funding thresholds triggering mandatory professional-investor participation; investor complaint procedure; and reporting forms.

Newcomer — Ultralight Aircraft Regulation

The decree of 28 July applying Law No. 467-VIIQD (Aviation) is the week's quiet surprise: it requires the Cabinet, within three months and in coordination with six agencies — the State Security Service, the Ministry of Digital Development, the Ministry of Justice, the Ministry of Youth and Sport, the Strategic Objects Protection Agency and the Tourism Agency — to approve rules on ultralight aircraft airspace use and flight operations. The rules must cover eight mandated areas including technical specifications, registration, operator age/training/medical fitness, permitted and prohibited zones (borders, military sites, aerodromes, special-regime areas), the commercial/non-commercial distinction, pre-flight safety, maintenance and inspection, and the rights and duties of operators. A further four-month deadline applies to proposals on liability for breach.

Why this matters for the firm: two entirely new regulated activities — crowdfunding platforms and ultralight aviation — are being built from a standing start, and in both cases the implementing rules are still unwritten. The three-to-six-month windows opening this week are, in practice, the consultation periods. Clients with an interest in either sector are better served by engaging now than by reading the finished rules in the new year.
Office of the President Fərmanlar — Decrees Thirteen decrees, 23–28 July 2026 — president.az

Grouped by subject matter rather than date. Every decree below applies a statute adopted by the Milli Majlis between 30 June and 14 July 2026, except where noted.

Finance, Tax & Capital Markets

Decree 24 July 2026
Application of the Law "On Crowdfunding" No. 450-VIIQ of 14 July 2026
Brings the new crowdfunding statute into operation. Powers under Arts. 3.2 and 18.8 retained by the President. Cabinet: four months to align presidential acts; three months to designate offshore zones. Central Bank: six months to issue six categories of implementing regulation (see lead story).
Decree 28 July 2026
Application of Law No. 445-VIIQD of 13 July 2026 amending the Tax Code
The President assumes the competence under the third sentence of Tax Code Art. 175.8, and the "relevant executive body" there is identified as the Cabinet of Ministers. The Cabinet has three months to approve the procedure and conditions under which non-oil, private-sector taxpayers may transfer VAT deposit amounts to the State Social Protection Fund. Presidential Decree No. 393 of 30 August 2000 is amended accordingly. Sections 1 and 3 enter into force on 1 January 2027.
Decree 27 July 2026
Application of Law No. 452-VIIQD of 14 July 2026 amending the Labour Code, the Civil Code and the laws on currency regulation, investment funds and the securities market
Despite the Labour Code in the title, the decree's operative content is capital-markets institutional: the President exercises the powers under Art. 9-4.5(2) of the Law on Investment Funds; the Ministry of Economy is designated the relevant executive body for those provisions; and the Ministry of Internal Affairs takes the functions under Art. 48.1-1 of that Law. The Cabinet is to resolve arising questions; no deadline is stated. The decree does not itself specify which Labour Code provisions were amended — the statute text should be consulted directly.
Decree 24 July 2026
Implementation of Law No. 469-VIIQD of 14 July 2026 amending the Law "On Execution"
Amends Presidential Decree No. 672 of 5 March 2002: the reference to the second sentence of Article 27.4 is deleted from paragraph 2.1, and paragraph 2.2-1 is repealed in full. Part one of Presidential Decree No. 406 of 5 June 2025 — which had implemented Law No. 196-VIIQD of 16 May 2025 on civil procedure and execution — is also repealed. The implementing scaffolding is being pruned, not extended.

Migration & Public Service

Decree 27 July 2026
Application of Law No. 465-VIIQD of 14 July 2026 amending the Migration Code and the laws on registration and state fees
Migration administration goes paperless. Approves the form of the decision on extending the temporary stay of foreigners and stateless persons; amends the State Migration Service's regulations to replace physical delivery with electronic issue — documents are executed to electronic-signature requirements, placed in the applicant's e-government portal cabinet and sent to the registered email address. Two earlier decrees (2009 and 2010) are amended to remove references to the paper decision and to update the migration information system.
Decree 27 July 2026
Amendment to the Regulation on competitions and interviews for candidates for service in the justice bodies
A one-word amendment to paragraph 1.10 of the Regulation approved by Presidential Decree No. 673 of 13 December 2007 (the word "state" is inserted). Minor, but it alters the Regulation's scope wording; anyone advising on entry to the justice service should read the amended paragraph in the consolidated text.
Decree 27 July 2026
Decision on extending the temporary stay of foreigners and stateless persons — approved form
The template approved under the Migration Code decree above, published as a separate instrument and made under Art. 39.4 of the Migration Code. The decision carries the State Emblem, a three-letter series "MOM" and a seven-digit number, the holder's identity and travel-document particulars, the extended period, the registration address, and a QR code for automated verification. It is issued in Azerbaijani and English, with names transliterated from the machine-readable zone of the travel document, and it expressly confers the right to exit Azerbaijan when presented with the passport.
Decree 24 July 2026
Amendment to the Statute of the Ministry of Economy
Amends the Statute approved by Presidential Decree No. 911 of 30 December 2019. Institutional-competence change; read together with the 27 July decree assigning the Ministry new investment-fund functions.

Transport & Aviation

Decree 28 July 2026
Application of Law No. 467-VIIQD of 14 July 2026 amending the Law "On Aviation"
Creates a regulatory regime for ultralight aircraft from a standing start. Cabinet: three months to approve rules on airspace use and flight operations, in coordination with six agencies, covering eight mandated subject areas; four months to submit proposals on liability for breach. See lead story. In force on issue.
Decree 28 July 2026
Application of Law No. 471-VIIQD of 14 July 2026 amending the Law "On Road Traffic"
The President assumes the competences previously vested in "the relevant executive body" under Articles 30 and 85 of the Law. The "relevant information system" is defined as the central information system of the Main State Road Police Directorate of the Ministry of Internal Affairs. Five textual amendments are made to the 1998 Regulation approved by Decree No. 24. Read with Cabinet Decisions Nos. 239 and 240 of 27 July.

Health, Housing & Social Protection

Decree 24 July 2026
Application of Law No. 464-VIIQD of 14 July 2026 amending the Laws "On Medical Insurance" and "On the Rights of Persons with Disabilities"
A structural reorganisation of mandatory medical insurance. Implementing authority passes from the State Agency on Mandatory Health Insurance to the Ministry of Labour and Social Protection of the Population, with a new State Medical Insurance and Expertise Agency established within the Ministry. A "Mandatory Medical Insurance" subsystem is to be formed within the Ministry's centralised electronic information system by 1 January 2027. The Cabinet has three months to submit draft rules on the classification and investment of MMI fund reserves. Conforming amendments to Decrees No. 502/2019 and No. 634/2019.
Decree 23 July 2026
Application of Law No. 444-VIIQD of 13 July 2026 amending the Law "On Psychiatric Care"
Puts the psychiatric care amendments into effect and amends Presidential Decree No. 574 of 3 September 2001. Relevant to health-sector regulatory work and to capacity, guardianship and involuntary-treatment questions.
Decree 23 July 2026
Application of Law No. 432-VIIQD of 30 June 2026 amending the Housing Code and the legislation on refugees and internally displaced persons
Implements the housing and IDP-status amendment package adopted at the close of the spring session. Read with the Presidential Order of the same date amending the 2004 State Programme on refugees and IDPs (Section 2) — two instruments touching resettlement entitlements on a single day.

Nazirlər Kabineti Cabinet of Ministers 10 Decisions & 6 Orders — nk.gov.az

The Cabinet's six Sərəncamlar this week are all of one kind — instruments securing the execution of a named presidential decree. They are the connective tissue of the July package and are listed separately below.

Decision No. 236 24 July 2026
Model structure and staff units of enterprises providing restoration, construction and management services in the liberated territories
The most commercially significant Cabinet instrument of the week. Approves a standard organisational template for the entities delivering reconstruction and estate-management services in the territories. Relevant to contractors, sub-contractors and anyone negotiating with these entities.
Decision No. 240 27 July 2026
Rules on refunding to the vehicle owner the sum paid for removal of a vehicle to, and its storage at, an impound lot
A new refund procedure where a vehicle has been impounded. Directly useful to road-traffic and administrative practice, and to insurers handling recovery claims.
Decision No. 239 27 July 2026
Amendments to certain Cabinet decisions pursuant to Presidential Decree No. 559 of 26 December 2025 (Law No. 307-VIIQD of 9 December 2025, "On Road Traffic")
The Cabinet-level limb of the road traffic reform, catching up with the December 2025 amendments.
Decision No. 233 24 July 2026
Repeal of Decisions No. 118 of 1 May 2014 and No. 38 of 29 January 2022

Regulatory Spotlight — Key Themes, Week 31 / 2026

1. A New Market Opens — Crowdfunding

Law 450-VIIQ is the first Azerbaijani statute to regulate crowdfunding as a distinct activity, and the 24 July decree starts the clock on the Central Bank's rulemaking. Until the CBAR acts land, the perimeter — who counts as an operator, what capital they must hold, what a retail investor may commit — is undefined. Corporate, banking and fintech teams should be scoping platform structures now and treating the next two quarters as the window in which this market's commercial parameters are settled.

2. Ultralight Aviation From Zero

The aviation decree of 28 July is the most striking new regulatory project of the week. An entire licensing-and-operations regime for ultralight aircraft must be built within three months, spanning registration, operator medical and training standards, geographic no-fly zones and the commercial/non-commercial divide — with liability rules following a month later. Any client operating, importing or insuring light aircraft should expect to be regulated for the first time, and should engage during the drafting window.

3. Migration Administration Goes Paperless

The 27 July Migration Code decree and its approved decision template replace physical documents with electronically signed instruments delivered to the applicant's e-government cabinet and email. The new temporary-stay extension decision carries an "MOM" series, a seven-digit number and a QR code, is bilingual, and expressly confers the right to exit on presentation with a passport. Immigration practices should update client guidance on what a valid extension now looks like — and stop expecting a paper decision.

4. Road Traffic — A Three-Instrument Package

The 28 July decree applying Law 471-VIIQD names the Road Police's central information system as the statutory "relevant information system" and pulls competences under Arts. 30 and 85 to the President. Cabinet Decisions 239 and 240 of 27 July complete the picture, the latter introducing a refund procedure for impound removal and storage charges. Administrative and insurance practices now have a concrete recovery route for wrongly impounded vehicles.

5. Health Insurance Administration Restructured

The 24 July decree on Law 464-VIIQD moves mandatory medical insurance out of the standalone State Agency and into the Ministry of Labour and Social Protection, creating a State Medical Insurance and Expertise Agency inside the Ministry. This is a change of counterparty as much as of competence: employers, insurers and providers should identify every contract, filing and correspondence channel currently addressed to the Agency. The 1 January 2027 systems deadline is the practical migration date.

6. Two Quiet Deregulatory Moves

Cabinet Decision 233 repeals outright the 2014 list of information systems required to connect to the e-government portal (with its technical requirements) and the 2022 procurement-portal integration thresholds. The execution decree of 24 July likewise deletes rather than adds, pruning Decree 672/2002 and revoking part of the 2025 implementing decree. In both areas the instruction is the same: verify that any compliance position or enforcement strategy still rests on a live provision.
