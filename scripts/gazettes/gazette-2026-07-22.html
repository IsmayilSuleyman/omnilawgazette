Parliament of Azerbaijan Milli Majlis Bills & Legislation — 14 July 2026

The Milli Majlis tabled a single, unusually large batch of 19 bills on 14 July 2026, touching the Constitutional Law on Normative Legal Acts and amending large parts of the Civil, Criminal, Tax, Labor, Land, Administrative Offenses and Migration Codes in one sitting. This is the broadest omnibus package logged since the Gazette began tracking in June. One new law entered the register during the reporting period: Law No. 447-VIIQD (13 July), amending the Law "On State Procurement".

The Cabinet issued ten Decisions across the period — Nos. 216–218 on 17 July and a seven-decision batch (Nos. 223–229) on 21–22 July — plus implementation Orders between 14 and 17 July. The 21 July batch is the standout: it includes the approval of national priority sectors for artificial intelligence development (No. 226).

Regulatory Spotlight — Key Themes, Weeks 29–30 / 2026

1. The 14 July Omnibus — Broadest Package Tracked to Date

Nineteen bills tabled in one sitting, spanning the Civil, Criminal, Tax, Labor, Land, Administrative Offenses and Migration Codes plus a new Crowdfunding law and Constitutional Law on Normative Acts amendment. The sheer breadth suggests a coordinated legislative push ahead of the autumn session. Corporate, tax, employment, and land-use practices should each nominate an owner to track the relevant sub-package through committee review — the Land Code changes (items 12–13) and the Tax/Migration/Procurement package (item 6) look most commercially consequential.

2. National AI Priority Sectors Designated

Cabinet Decision No. 226 (21 July) approves the national priority sectors for artificial intelligence development and names the responsible state agencies. This is the first sector-level operationalisation of Azerbaijan's AI strategy at Cabinet level — clients in technology, data-intensive services, and any sector that ends up on the priority list should expect follow-on regulation, incentives, and procurement activity. Obtain the full sector list from e-qanun.az once gazetted.

3. Azerbaijan–Germany Strategic Agenda

The 21 July state visit to Berlin produced a Joint Declaration on a Strategic Agenda for the bilateral partnership with Germany. While not itself a normative act, declarations of this kind typically precede sector agreements (energy, infrastructure, investment protection). German-linked clients and inbound-investment practices should monitor for implementing instruments in the coming months.

4. Crowdfunding Law & Procurement Amendment

The standalone "On Crowdfunding" bill would be Azerbaijan's first dedicated regime for crowdfunding platforms — fintech clients should track it through subsequent readings. Meanwhile, Law No. 447-VIIQD (13 July) has already amended the Law "On State Procurement", and the 14 July omnibus (item 6) proposes further procurement changes — suppliers to the state should review both together.
