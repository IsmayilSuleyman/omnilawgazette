İqtisadi Kabinet / Nazirlər Kabineti Cabinet of Ministers Decisions & Orders — 29–31 July 2026

With parliament in recess, the Cabinet of Ministers was the most active legal-instrument source this week, issuing ten Decisions (No. 242–251) and twelve Orders (No. 536s–571s) between 29 and 31 July. A large share of the Orders formally implement presidential decrees signed in the second half of July, several of which are not yet reflected in president.az's own public category pages (see note below).

Regulatory Spotlight — Key Themes, Week 31 / 2026

1. Government Carries the Load During Recess

With the Milli Majlis in summer recess, all legal-instrument activity this week ran through the executive branch: the Cabinet issued ten Decisions and twelve Orders in three days (29–31 July). Firms tracking the legislative calendar should expect this pattern — Cabinet-driven implementation rather than new statutes — to continue until the autumn parliamentary session opens.

2. A Wave of Late-July Presidential Decrees

At least ten presidential decrees/orders (No. 726–740 range, plus No. 1100 and 1103) were signed between 15 and 27 July and are only visible indirectly, through Cabinet implementing orders. They touch health insurance, disability rights, housing/IDP status, general education, public procurement, customs tariffs, and anti-trafficking policy — a broad cross-section worth flagging to clients in the affected sectors even though the primary texts are not yet easily accessible.

3. Caspian Renewable Energy Zoning Continues

Decision No. 251 (31 July) designates a new Caspian Sea renewable-energy zone, extending the land- and now sea-based zoning programme seen in earlier issues (e.g. Hacıqabul and Jabrayil in June). Energy and infrastructure practices should track the cumulative footprint of these zoning decisions.

4. Housing Code & IDP/Refugee Status Amendments

Order No. 536s implements Decree No. 732, amending the Housing Code and IDP/refugee status laws, alongside a related state programme on refugee/IDP housing improvement and employment (Order No. 537s / Presidential Order No. 1103). Practices advising on housing, resettlement, or social-benefits matters should watch for the consolidated texts once published.
